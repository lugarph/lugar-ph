Why Lugar.ph

Jeepneys carry most commuters in Philippine cities, but their routes are hard to learn if you're new. They're written in city route plans and ordinances, or passed along by word of mouth. Lugar.ph gathers what's published and what volunteers have mapped in OpenStreetMap into one free trip planner, so anyone can get around like a local.

Best estimates, not official timetables

Some lines are mapped street by street; others are drawn from published route plans and marked as estimates. Routes change, so check the signboard and ask the driver before you ride. If something is wrong, leave a note on the line in the app so the next rider knows.
